# UN-Mayzing brand notes

## Brand

- **Display name:** UN-Mayzing
- **Tagline:** Untangle your entertainment.
- **One-line description:** A controller-first living-room interface that brings TV, streaming, games, live TV and smart-home controls together in one place.
- **Short pitch:** UN-Mayzing turns the small PC behind your TV into a unified home-entertainment screen. Use one controller or remote to launch your media, games and home controls without juggling separate interfaces.
- **Audience:** People building a flexible, PC-based living-room setup; open-source contributors interested in media, gaming, Linux kiosk systems, integrations and controller-first UX.
- **Tone:** Approachable, capable, slightly playful; avoid sounding like a commercial streaming service.

## Visual direction

- **Primary background:** deep charcoal/navy (`#171C24`)
- **Primary accent:** mint green (`#A5E9C8`)
- **Light foreground:** white (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Secondary foreground:** cool grey (`#9AA7B5`)
- **Logo concept:** a simple maze path resolving into a play symbol or doorway; clear at small app-icon sizes.
- **Wordmark:** preserve the distinctive capitalisation `UN-Mayzing`. Keep the hyphen in editorial branding; technical slugs may use lowercase `un-mayzing`.

## Messaging

- **Hero:** Your whole living room. One place.
- **Supporting line:** TV, movies, games, live TV and smart-home controls in one controller-first interface.
- **Tagline:** Untangle your entertainment.
- **Open-source framing:** Built in the open for people who want their living-room setup to work their way.

## Naming and legal status

The public web search so far did not surface a clear exact-match software project for `UN-Mayzing` or `UN-Mayzeing`. A similar-sounding `Mayzing` brand is already used in ecommerce software, so potential confusion should be considered. This is not a legal clearance.

- Domain availability has **not** been confirmed. Check `unmayzing.com`, `un-mayzing.com`, `unmayzing.au` and `un-mayzing.au` directly with a registrar before choosing.
- Search Australian Trade Mark Search for `UN-MAYZING`, `UNMAYZING`, `UN MAYZING`, `MAYZING` and similar-sounding variants, including relevant software, entertainment and technology goods/services.
- Consider target countries as well as Australia if the project will be promoted internationally.
- Do not announce domain availability or trademark clearance until verified.
